# Original Raw Curve Figures

Figure1b: XJTU batch-1 battery-1, physical cycle10, fixed before rendering. Upper panels show the complete charge stage; blue points identify the paper voltage/current windows. The voltage panel uses the public reader's I>=3.9A CC gate. Lower panels overlay every valid physical-cycle window with physical cycle as color. Sample index is the within-charge or within-selected-window sample index, not elapsed time. This figure deliberately uses the stated paper4.2V limit; the exploratory4.195V feature-audit boundary is exported as a separate source column and is not substituted in this plot. The original paper's exact example-cell identity is unreported, so this is an independent raw-data illustration, not pixel identity.

FigureS4: The first battery of each of the six XJTU batches, matching the declared selection in Supplementary FigS4. All physical cycles are drawn and color runs from early (purple) to late (yellow) within each battery. Voltage, current, and temperature use complete cycles, including charge/rest/discharge stages, with uniform within-cycle display sampling only; source counts are retained in figureS4_display_sampling.csv. The long initial capacity-test cycle is deliberately retained; its full sample-index extent differs from the cropped regular-cycle range visible in the original paper. Discharge capacity is plotted for every physical cycle. In batches4-6, incomplete discharges are not full-capacity measurements: white markers identify labelled periodic capacity tests and the orange line is the public reader's interpolated trajectory. Batch3 is described as fully discharged to2.5V in Note1.3 and its first cell has only one labelled capacity test; its raw discharge values are shown without an invented interpolation. This resolves a discrepancy with the broader batches3-6 statement in the S4 caption, and agrees with the absence of a red line on the original S4 batch3 panel. The exported capacity CSV distinguishes each quantity. No laboratory hardware or new cell tests are claimed.
